KG500/KG500A型露天潜孔钻车

KG500/KG500A Down The Hole Drill Rig For Open Use

KG500/KG500A型露天潜孔钻车液压系统采用高强度大排量铸铁齿轮泵，保证产品质量，同时具有使用寿命长、产品性能稳定的优势；行走采用加宽单筋板工程履带，具有接地比压高、越野性能强等优点；导轨采用高强度、大断面铝合金型材，推进链条采用加大单排滚子链，同时滑板压条采用耐磨材料，保证产品使用寿命；推进和回转采用两档速度，保证钻车可钻凿不同岩层，具有高效的凿岩速度；大减速比的回转器为钻车提供高达7000Nm以上的回转扭矩，推进马达采用高压力柱塞马达，提高提升力和可靠性。可广泛使用在较大孔径钻凿的工地施工。KG500A潜孔钻车是在KG500基础上增加摆臂油缸（角度）。

The hydraulic system of KG500/KG500A down the hole drill rig for open use adopts high strength and large displacement cast iron gear pump to ensure product quality, and has the advantages of long service life and stable product performance.  When walking, it adopts widened single-rib slab engineering track, which has the advantages of high specific grounding pressure and strong off-road performance.  The guide rail adopts high-strength and large-section aluminum alloy profile, the push chain adopts single-row roller chain, and the slide plate layering adopts wear-resistant material to ensure the service life of the product;  Propulsion and rotation adopt two speed, to ensure that the drill car can drill different rock strata, with efficient drilling speed;  The large reduction ratio gyroscope provides more than 7,000 nm of rotary torque, and the propulsion motor uses high pressure plunger motor to improve lifting force and reliability.  Can be widely used in the construction site of large aperture drilling.  KG500A DTH drill is based on KG500 increase swing arm cylinder (Angle).
